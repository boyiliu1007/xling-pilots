## Limitations
  - One model, one prompt, one seed; n=8. COPSD set = 500 items, R3 only 100 items (wide intervals).
  - math-verify can miss equivalences (e.g. unusual LaTeX, text answers, intervals vs sets) or accept loose ones; the gold 'Answer' check above bounds this only for the gold side.
  - Non-thinking answers are read from the last \boxed{}; thinking answers only from text after </think>.
  - Target-language questions are COPSD's machine translations (not human); English is the original.
  - Precision of the majority answer is measured against gold; it says nothing about the quality of the reasoning trace a self-distillation teacher would provide.
